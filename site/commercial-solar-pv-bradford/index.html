Manningham Concrete Ltd · Bradford

Commercial Solar PV Installation

A 50kW roof mounted solar PV system on the head office at Manningham Concrete Ltd, installed in two weeks and now covering around two thirds of the site’s electricity.

ClientManningham Concrete Ltd
LocationBradford
SectorCommercial
CertificationMCS and NICEIC certificates

About the Project

How We Helped

The roof takes 110 panels running into SolaX inverters, sized from the site’s own consumption data rather than roof space alone, so as much of the generation as possible gets used on site instead of being exported.

In the first ten weeks after switch on the system generated 10.25MWh, and 71% of that was used on site as it was produced. That figure is the one that matters: every unit used on site is a unit not bought from the grid at business rates, with network charges and levies on top.

  • System design and modelling from the site’s own consumption data
  • Distribution network operator application
  • Roof survey and mounting system design
  • Installation of 110 roof mounted panels
  • SolaX inverters and switchgear
  • DC and AC containment, cabling and isolation
  • Generation monitoring
  • MCS certificate issued on completion, as required for Smart Export Guarantee payments
  • NICEIC installation certificate supplied upon completion
