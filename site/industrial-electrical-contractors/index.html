Overview

Electrical Work That Keeps Production Moving

From new industrial units to busy production floors, we deliver electrical installations that are safe, compliant and built around the way your site operates. We design, install, test and maintain power and lighting for manufacturers, distribution businesses and engineering firms.

Every project is planned to protect your output. We arrange shutdowns around shifts and weekends, give you a single point of contact throughout and hand over full NICEIC certification at the end.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Can you work around our production schedule?

Yes. We plan power shutdowns and disruptive work around your shifts, and regularly work evenings and weekends so production keeps running.

Do you install power for new machinery?

Yes. We assess what the machine needs and what your existing supply can handle, then install the distribution, cabling and isolation, and test and certify every new circuit.

Do you work outside Yorkshire?

Yes. We are based in Wakefield and deliver industrial projects across the UK.

Do you offer maintenance after the installation?

Yes. We provide planned preventative maintenance and reactive support, so the team that installed your system can look after it.
