Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

How much can a commercial solar PV system save?

Savings vary depending on energy usage, system size and operating hours. A well designed solar PV system can significantly reduce reliance on grid electricity and lower long term energy costs.

Can battery storage be added to an existing solar PV system?

Yes. Battery storage can often be integrated with existing solar installations, allowing organisations to store excess energy for use during peak periods or outside daylight hours.

Do you provide maintenance and ongoing support?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance, testing and support services to help ensure your renewable energy systems continue operating safely and efficiently.
