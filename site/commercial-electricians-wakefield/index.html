Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Do you cover the whole Wakefield district?

Yes. We work right across the district, including the city centre, Ossett, Horbury, Normanton, Castleford, Pontefract and the business parks by the M1 and M62. We also work throughout Yorkshire and the rest of the UK.

Can you work outside our opening hours?

Yes. We regularly work early mornings, evenings and weekends so shops, offices and production lines are not disrupted.

Are you NICEIC approved?

Yes. Econ Electrical is a NICEIC Approved Contractor, MCS certified for solar PV and battery storage, and SafeContractor accredited. Our NICEIC and MCS registrations can be checked online.

How quickly will we hear back?

A member of our team will be in touch within one working day of your enquiry. If it is urgent, call us on 01924 677085.
