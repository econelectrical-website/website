Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Is it really free?

Yes, and there is no obligation at the end of it. We do these because a site we have surveyed properly is a site we can quote accurately, and because roughly half the buildings we look at turn out to be worth doing. The report is yours either way.

What if the answer is that solar is not worth it?

Then the report says so, and it says why. A north facing roof, a short lease or a site that only draws power at night can all make the numbers not work. We would rather tell you that than sell you something that disappoints you.

We lease the building. Is there any point?

Often yes, but the landlord has to agree, because the system is attached to their building for 25 years. It is worth knowing early, which is why we ask about the lease on the form. Shorter leases can still work through a power purchase agreement.

Why do you need a year of bills?

Because the saving depends on when you use power, not just how much. A site running two shifts uses far more of its own generation than one that shuts at four, and that changes the payback by years. A single bill tells us a quarter of the story.

What is a Letter of Authority?

A short form that lets us ask your supplier for your half hourly usage data. It is read only and covers usage, nothing else. You can withdraw it at any time, and it saves you digging a year of bills out of a filing cabinet.

How long does the site visit take?

About an hour for most sites. We look at the roof, the incoming supply and the distribution boards. We do not need to interrupt what you are doing.
