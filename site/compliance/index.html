Thermal Imaging

Thermal imaging surveys provide a non invasive way to identify hidden electrical issues before they result in equipment failure, downtime or safety risks. By detecting abnormal heat patterns, we can highlight potential faults that may not be visible during standard inspections.

This proactive approach allows organisations to address developing issues early, helping reduce maintenance costs and improve the reliability of critical electrical assets.

  • Thermal surveys
  • Hotspot detection
  • Risk identification
  • Asset monitoring
  • Preventative inspections
  • Detailed reporting

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

How often is electrical testing required?

Testing intervals vary depending on the type of premises and how the installation is used. We can advise on the appropriate schedule for your organisation.

What is an EICR?

An Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) assesses the condition and safety of an electrical installation, highlighting any defects or areas requiring attention.

Why is thermal imaging important?

Thermal imaging can identify developing faults before they lead to equipment failure, downtime or costly repairs, making it a valuable preventative maintenance tool.
