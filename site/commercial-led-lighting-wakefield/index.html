Cut the Cost of Keeping the Lights On

In most warehouses, factories and offices the lights run for long hours every day, and older fluorescent, sodium or metal halide fittings burn through energy while they do it. Swapping them for well designed LED lighting is one of the quickest ways a business can bring its bills down.

We design the new layout around your racking, workstations and walkways, add sensors where they will save you money, and install out of hours so you never have to stop. When Saint Flooring Group opened their new warehouse in Wakefield, we fitted LED high bays throughout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

How much could an LED upgrade save us?

It depends on your current lighting and how long it runs. At BM Packaging in Huddersfield, our upgrade cut energy costs by 55%. We show you the expected savings before you commit.

Will we have to close while you install?

No. We regularly install in the evenings and at weekends so your business keeps running.

Can you reuse our existing circuits?

Often, yes. We check their condition during the survey and reuse them where it is safe to do so.

Can our emergency lighting be done at the same time?

Yes, and it makes sense to. While we are working on your lighting we can check emergency lighting coverage and swap older fittings for LED.
