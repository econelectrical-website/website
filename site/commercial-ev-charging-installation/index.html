Charging That Fits Your Site

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

How many chargers can our building support?

It depends on your incoming supply and how much of it is already in use. We check your maximum demand during the survey and, where needed, use load management to fit more chargers on the same supply.

Which chargers do you install?

We install smart chargers from established manufacturers, choosing models that suit your vehicles and your site and that meet the GB smart charge point regulations where they apply.

Do we need permission from the network operator?

Larger installations may need to be notified to, or approved by, the Distribution Network Operator. We check this during the survey and manage the application for you.

Can we charge drivers for using the chargers?

Yes. Smart chargers let you control who can charge and can pass the cost of electricity on to drivers if you choose.
