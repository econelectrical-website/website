Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

How many chargers can our premises support?

It depends on your incoming supply and how much of it you already use. We check your maximum demand during the survey and, where it helps, use load management so more chargers can share the same supply.

Can you install rapid chargers for our fleet?

Yes. We install DC rapid chargers as well as AC chargers, including four DC chargers for Devitech in Reading.

Can we charge staff or visitors to use them?

Yes. Smart chargers let you control who can charge and pass the cost of the electricity on to drivers if you choose.

Do we need approval from the network operator?

Larger installations may need to be notified to, or approved by, Northern Powergrid, the network operator for Wakefield. We find out during the survey and deal with the application on your behalf.
