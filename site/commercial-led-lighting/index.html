Overview

Lower Bills, Better Light

Lighting is one of the biggest energy costs for most commercial and industrial buildings. Replacing older fluorescent, sodium or metal halide fittings with LED cuts that cost dramatically, reduces maintenance and gives people better, more even light to work in.

At BM Packaging in Huddersfield, our high bay LED upgrade delivered a 55% reduction in energy costs and a 40% increase in lux levels, installed out of hours so the factory never had to stop.

Designed for the Space

Fittings, spacing and mounting heights planned around racking, workstations and walkways.

Smart Controls

Presence and daylight sensors that switch off or dim lights when they are not needed.

No Downtime

Out of hours installation, with MEWPs for safe access at height.

Lower Carbon

Less energy, longer life and far fewer replacements at height.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

How much can an LED upgrade save?

It depends on your existing lighting and how long it runs. At BM Packaging, our upgrade delivered a 55% reduction in energy costs.

Can you install without stopping production?

Yes. We regularly install out of hours, in the evenings and at weekends, so your site keeps running.

Can the new lights use our existing circuits?

Often, yes. We check the condition of your existing circuits during the survey and reuse them where it is safe to do so.

Can you upgrade our emergency lighting at the same time?

Yes. An upgrade is the ideal time to check emergency lighting coverage and replace older fittings with LED.
