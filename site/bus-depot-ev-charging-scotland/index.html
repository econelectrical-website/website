About the Project

How We Helped

Containment went up first, set out with a laser level and run the full length of the depot wall, then the LV cabling was pulled in and terminated into the charger power cabinets standing on new plinths.

Most of this one was outdoors and the site stayed in use throughout, so the work was staged in sections behind barriers. It took some grinding to get over the line, and the finish is the same standard as the London depot.

  • Electrical subcontract delivery to the main contractor
  • Setting out and installation of all galvanised containment
  • Installation of LV cabling to every charging position
  • Installation of charger power cabinets
  • Cable terminations at the LV panel and at each cabinet
  • Work staged in sections around a depot that stayed in use
